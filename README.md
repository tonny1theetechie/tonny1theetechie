## Featured Projects

### MediMatch Kenya
A full healthcare technology ecosystem combating Antimicrobial 
Resistance (AMR) in Kenya. Serves three user groups:

- **Healthcare Providers** - intelligent drug matching, interaction 
  checking and AMR resistance map
- **Patients** - medication reminders, adherence tracking and 
  clinic messaging
- **General Public** - 80+ health articles, 30+ interactive 
  modules and quizzes
- **Offline-first** architecture for low-connectivity environments 
  across Kenya

[Support Portal](https://customer-support-web-zeta.vercel.app) | 
[Admin Panel](https://medimatch-kenya-admin.vercel.app)  
`React Native` `Expo` `Firebase` `Cloudinary` `JavaScript`


### NyumbaniFix
A mobile marketplace connecting Kenyan homeowners with local 
service providers such as electricians, plumbers, and cleaners.

- Browse and hire verified service providers
- Job request creation, tracking and management
- Ratings, reviews and push notifications

`React Native` `Expo` `NativeWind` `Django` `PostgreSQL` 
`Firebase Auth` `Vercel`
